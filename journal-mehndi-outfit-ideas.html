Mehndi & Mayun Outfit Ideas for Brides and Guests

Bright yellows, greens and playful gota work — ideas for every pre-wedding function.

Colours that celebrate

Mayun calls for yellow and mustard, while mehndi is all about greens, oranges and multicolour. Pick one bold colour and balance it with gold accents.

Comfort first

Pre-wedding functions are full of dancing. Choose lighter lawn, cotton net or chiffon, and a flowy gharara or sharara for easy movement.

Finish with the details

Gota borders, tassels and mirror work add a festive touch without the weight of full bridal embroidery.
